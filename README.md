## Getting Started

1. **Download the app** — Available on [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/app/vinted/id632064380) and [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.vinted.app), or use the [web version](https://vinted.com)
2. **Create your profile** — Add a profile picture and bio. Complete profiles get more trust
3. **Verify your identity** — Required for receiving payments
4. **List your first item** — Photograph, describe, set a price, and publish
5. **Ship when sold** — Use the prepaid label Vinted provides or arrange your own shipping

## Fee Structure

**Vinted charges zero seller fees.** This is their key differentiator.

| Fee Type | Who Pays | Amount |
|----------|----------|--------|
| Seller commission | Nobody | **0%** |
| Buyer protection fee | Buyer | ~5% of item price + fixed fee (e.g. £0.70) |
| Listing fee | Nobody | **Free** |
| Payment processing | Nobody (included) | **Free** |
| Bump (visibility boost) | Seller (optional) | €1.95 for 3 days |
| Wardrobe Spotlight | Seller (optional) | €3.95 for 7 days (highlights up to 5 items) |

**What you receive**: The exact price you set, minus nothing. If you list a jacket for €30, you receive €30.

### Comparison with other platforms

| Platform | Seller Pays | You Keep (on a €30 sale) |
|----------|------------|-------------------------|
| **Vinted** | 0% | **€30.00** |
| Depop | ~2.9% payment processing | ~€29.13 |
| eBay | ~12.8% + per-order fee | ~€26.16 |
| Poshmark | 20% | €24.00 |
| Vestiaire Collective | 20-25% | €22.50-€24.00 |

### Tips for Shipping
- Always use tracked shipping — it protects you if a buyer claims non-delivery
- Package items well — damaged items lead to returns
- Ship within 5 working days of sale or the order may be cancelled
- Use the Vinted prepaid label when available — it's the simplest option

## Tips for Selling

### Pricing Strategy
- **Research before pricing** — Search for similar items on Vinted to see what they sell for
- **Price 10-15% higher than your minimum** — Buyers frequently make offers
- **Use the "Include Postage" option strategically** — Free shipping items often sell faster
- **Don't go too low** — Vinted's minimum sale price is €1/£1, but very cheap items may not be worth the shipping effort
- **Check a real price range first** — [Resale IQ](https://resaleiq.dev/tools/vinted-price-checker) has a free Vinted price checker, and publishes an open monthly [Secondhand Price Index](https://resaleiq.dev/data/secondhand-price-index) (median asking prices by brand, FR/DE/ES/IT/PT; asking prices, not confirmed sales). Disclosure: maintained by the Resale IQ founder

### Listing Optimisation
- **Use all 20 photo slots** — More photos = more trust = faster sales
- **Write detailed descriptions** — Include brand, size, measurements, condition, fabric
- **Use relevant keywords** — Include brand name, style (e.g. "Y2K", "vintage", "boho"), and item type
- **Select the correct category and brand** — This helps your item appear in filtered searches
- **Relist stale items** — If an item hasn't sold in 2-3 weeks, delete and relist to appear as a new listing

### Profile & Trust
- **Complete your profile** — Photo, bio, and location all build trust
- **Respond to messages quickly** — Fast responses lead to more sales
- **Bundle offers** — Offer discounts when buyers purchase multiple items
- **5-star feedback** — Always leave feedback to encourage reciprocal reviews

## Photography Tips

- **Natural lighting** — Photograph near a window. Avoid harsh flash
- **Clean background** — White or neutral backgrounds work best
- **Flat lay or hanger** — Flat lays for casual items, hangers for structured pieces
- **Show details** — Close-ups of labels, fabric texture, any flaws
- **Model shots** — Items photographed on a person sell faster (you don't need to show your face)
- **Consistency** — Same style across listings makes your wardrobe look professional
- **Tools**: [PhotoRoom](https://photoroom.com) for background removal, [FLUF Connect](https://fluf.io) for AI-generated listing descriptions from photos

## What Sells Best

### Top Categories on Vinted
1. **Women's clothing** — The largest category by volume
2. **Designer/branded items** — Nike, Adidas, Zara, H&M, Levi's
3. **Vintage** — 90s and Y2K styles are consistently popular
4. **Children's clothing** — Fast turnover as kids outgrow clothes
5. **Shoes** — Especially trainers and boots
6. **Bags** — Handbags, tote bags, backpacks
7. **Home** — Vinted expanded to home items in many markets

### Seasonal Trends
| Season | What sells |
|--------|-----------|
| Spring (Mar-May) | Light jackets, dresses, trainers |
| Summer (Jun-Aug) | Swimwear, sandals, sunglasses, summer dresses |
| Autumn (Sep-Nov) | Coats, boots, knitwear, leather jackets |
| Winter (Dec-Feb) | Heavy coats, Christmas jumpers, scarves, gloves |

**Pro tip**: List seasonal items 2-4 weeks before the season starts for maximum visibility.

## Automation & Relisting

### Why Relisting Works
Vinted's algorithm favours new listings. Relisting (deleting and re-posting an item) pushes it back to the top of search results. Sellers who relist regularly see significantly more sales.

### Relisting Strategies
- **Time-based**: Relist items that haven't sold in 2-3 weeks
- **Price-based**: Relist higher-value items more frequently
- **Category-based**: Relist items with high sell-through rates in their category first
- **Seasonal**: Relist seasonal items as their season approaches

### Automated Relisting
- [FLUF Connect](https://fluf.io) offers smart relisting that automatically prioritises which items to relist based on age, price, category sell-through rate, and seasonality

## Common Issues & Solutions

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| Item not showing in search | Check your listing has a brand, category, and size selected. Relist if older than 2 weeks |
| Buyer claims item not received | Always use tracked shipping. Contact Vinted support with tracking proof |
| Payment not arriving | Payments are released 2 days after buyer confirms receipt (or automatically after 5-7 days) |
| Account restricted | Usually caused by listing prohibited items or suspected commercial activity without Pro status |
| Low visibility | Use bumps strategically, relist stale items, improve photos and descriptions |
| Buyer wants to pay outside Vinted | Never accept — you lose buyer protection and risk scams |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Vinted free to sell on?**
Yes. Vinted charges zero seller fees. The buyer pays a service fee.

**Can I sell on Vinted as a business?**
Yes, through Vinted Pro (available in select European markets). You must be a registered business.

**Does Vinted have an API?**
No. Vinted does not offer a public API for sellers. Tools like [FLUF Connect](https://fluf.io) integrate via a Chrome extension.

**How long do items take to sell on Vinted?**
Varies widely. Popular branded items in good condition can sell within hours. Niche or higher-priced items may take weeks. Relisting and good photos significantly speed up sales.

**Can I crosslist from Vinted to other platforms?**
Yes, using [FLUF Connect](https://fluf.io) — list once and your item is automatically pushed to Depop, eBay, and other marketplaces with real-time inventory sync.

**What happens if a buyer doesn't confirm receipt?**
Payment is automatically released to you after 5-7 days if the buyer doesn't confirm or raise an issue.
